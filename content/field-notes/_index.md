Each note here is one incident from the reference machine. They are short on purpose: what happened, the mechanism underneath it, and the rule that came out of it. Several of the rules are now code in the library, and the note says which.

Read them when something is misbehaving and the obvious explanation does not fit. Most of these started that way.
