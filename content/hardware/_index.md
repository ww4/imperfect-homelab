Storage connectivity decides whether a machine can be this homelab; compute barely matters, and power is the real price. The reference box is a 2014 desktop CPU with twelve drives hanging off it, and the hardware incidents in its history that cost real time were drives dropping off a bus, not slow cores. So this chapter spends its space on ports, bays and watts, and dispatches the CPU question in a sentence. The last page is the evaluation process the reference machine's owner uses on salvage: a decision in under an hour of hands-on time, most of it unattended, with a written card at the end.
